Privacy Policy

Learn how SA Local Business collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SA Local Business, we may collect information needed to provide and operate the application.

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account information

Location Information

We may collect location information to support location-based features, including helping users discover businesses and displaying relevant map or location information.

Pictures and Photos

Users and businesses may upload pictures or photos to profiles and listings. Photos that form part of a public business listing may be visible to other users.

Password Information

Your password is used to secure your account. Passwords should never be shared with other users.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and operate SA Local Business
  • Display business profiles and listings
  • Help users find businesses
  • Provide location and map-related features
  • Allow businesses to manage their profiles
  • Allow users to contact businesses
  • Display photos associated with business profiles or listings
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Help you reset or recover your password
  • Maintain the security of the application
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of our services

3. Business Listings and Public Information

If you create a business profile or listing, information you choose to publish may be visible to other users.

This may include your business name, phone number, location, pictures, and other information you provide for your business listing.

Please remember: Do not upload information that you do not want publicly displayed as part of a business listing.

4. Location Information

Location information may be used to provide location-based functionality, including helping users discover businesses near a particular location.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. If you disable location permissions, some location-based features may not function correctly.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee that information will always be completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when necessary to:

  • Provide the application's services
  • Operate and maintain our systems
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal requirements
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the application

Information that you intentionally publish as part of a public business listing may be visible to other users.

7. Password Reset

If you forget your SA Local Business password, use the Forgot Password or Reset Password option available on the login screen.

Password Reset Steps

  1. Open the SA Local Business app.
  2. Go to the Login screen.
  3. Select Forgot Password or Reset Password.
  4. Enter the email address associated with your account.
  5. Check your email for the password-reset instructions.
  6. Follow the instructions to create a new secure password.
  7. Return to SA Local Business and sign in with your new password.

If you do not receive the password-reset email, check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the correct email address.

Security reminder: Never share your password or password-reset link with another person.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our services.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your account or personal information, please contact us using the contact information below.

When a deletion request is processed, information may be deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain by law.

10. Children's Privacy

SA Local Business is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can review the request.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, account deletion, or password recovery, please contact us.
